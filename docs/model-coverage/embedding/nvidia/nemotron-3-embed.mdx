Nemotron 3 Embed models encode multilingual text queries and documents for dense
retrieval. They use Ministral3 with bidirectional attention and average pooling.

## Released Checkpoints

Choose the checkpoint for your workflow. The linked model cards document inference,
query and passage prefixes, training data, and evaluation.

| Variant | Hugging Face Checkpoint | Embedding Dimensions | Example Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1B | [nvidia/Nemotron-3-Embed-1B-BF16](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Nemotron-3-Embed-1B-BF16) | 2,048 | Trainable student in text distillation |
| 8B | [nvidia/Nemotron-3-Embed-8B-BF16](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Nemotron-3-Embed-8B-BF16) | 4,096 | Frozen teacher in text distillation |

## Fine-Tuning Resources

For contrastive fine-tuning and data preparation, follow the
[retrieval fine-tuning guide](/recipes-e2e-examples/retrieval-finetune).
Preserve the model's average pooling, normalization, and query and passage prefixes.

The [text distillation example](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Automodel/blob/main/examples/retrieval/distillation/nemotron3_embed_1b_distill.yaml)
continues training the released 1B student with the frozen 8B teacher. A trainable
projection maps student embeddings to the teacher's 4,096-dimensional space for
the distillation losses. The example does not perform the pruning stages used to
create the 1B checkpoint. Follow the
[example README](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Automodel/blob/main/examples/retrieval/distillation/README.md)
for dataset requirements and launch instructions.

For image and text documents, refer to
[Nemotron 3.5 Embed](/model-coverage/embedding-models/nvidia/Nemotron-3.5-Embed).
