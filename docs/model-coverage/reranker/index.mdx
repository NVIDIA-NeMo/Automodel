Reranking models score a query and document together to reorder candidates from a retriever.
Fine-tune a released reranker to adapt its relevance scores to your data. For first-stage
candidate retrieval, refer to [Embedding Models](/model-coverage/embedding-models/overview).

## Supported Models

Choose a released checkpoint by publisher. Each page describes the model and available fine-tuning resources.

| Publisher | Reranking Model | Inputs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NVIDIA | [llama-nemotron-rerank-1b-v2](/model-coverage/reranking-models/nvidia/llama-nemotron-rerank-1b-v2) | Text query-document pairs |
| NVIDIA | [Nemotron-3.5-Rerank](/model-coverage/reranking-models/nvidia/Nemotron-3.5-Rerank) | Text queries; text, image, or combined documents |
| Qwen | [Qwen3-Reranker-4B](/model-coverage/reranking-models/qwen/Qwen3-Reranker-4B) | Text query-document pairs |

## Fine-Tune a Reranker

Follow the [Embedding and Reranking Fine-Tuning Guide](/recipes-e2e-examples/retrieval-finetune)
for data preparation, configuration, training, and checkpoint export. Use the selected model's
tokenizer and input formatting, including its scoring head or yes/no scoring behavior.

The [Qwen3-Reranker-4B example](/model-coverage/reranking-models/qwen/Qwen3-Reranker-4B)
loads the released checkpoint directly. For multimodal documents, refer to the
[Nemotron 3.5 Rerank example](/model-coverage/reranking-models/nvidia/Nemotron-3.5-Rerank).

## Train from a Base Model

To create a custom reranker from Llama or Ministral weights, follow
[Train Retrieval Models from Base Checkpoints](/recipes-e2e-examples/retrieval-base-models).
These examples initialize a scoring model from a language or vision-language checkpoint.
They do not reproduce a named, released reranker.
