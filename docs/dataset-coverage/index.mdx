Explore datasets hosted on the Hugging Face Hub and referenced by NeMo AutoModel
training examples. Each card explains the dataset's task, shows a synthetic record,
and describes its source schema and splits before linking to AutoModel recipes.

Cards also cover companion corpora and named sources within mixtures. Synthetic
examples are invented illustrations, not copied training rows. For local data formats
and synthetic benchmark loaders, see the [dataset integration guides](/datasets/overview).
