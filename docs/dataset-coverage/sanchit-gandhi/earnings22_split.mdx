## Use with NeMo AutoModel

| Workflow | Example Setup | Recipe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mixed-Corpus Speech Fine-Tuning | Qwen2.5-Omni 3B; six English sources | [View YAML](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Automodel/blob/03429168a27bfffd10d109d2adf436b49f1c82a7/examples/audio_finetune/qwen2_5_omni_asr/multi_en_sft_3b.yaml) |

**Adapter:** [`make_multi_en_asr_dataset`](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Automodel/blob/03429168a27bfffd10d109d2adf436b49f1c82a7/nemo_automodel/components/datasets/audio/multi_en.py).

The mixture maps `sentence` to `text`, adds a source label, normalizes transcripts to uppercase, removes punctuation except apostrophes, and decodes audio lazily at 16,000 Hz.  The recipe uses AMI, Earnings22, VoxPopuli, GigaSpeech, SPGISpeech, and LibriSpeech together. Override `dataset.sources` to change the mixture.

The mixture uses `train` from this source. The recipe does not configure a validation dataset; duration and empty-text filtering can reduce the loaded count.
